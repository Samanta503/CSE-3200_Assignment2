# Demo Video Script

1. Start the application.
2. Show the Finance and Debt Tracker tabs.
3. In Finance tab, add one income transaction.
4. Add one expense transaction.
5. Show Total Income, Total Expenses, and Net Balance update.
6. Delete a selected transaction.
7. Go to Debt Tracker tab.
8. Add a debt:
   - Name: Rahim Bhai
   - Type: I Owe
   - Original Amount: 50000
   - Interest Rate: 5
   - Due Date: future date
9. Show the debt appears with Pending status.
10. Select the debt and click Make Payment.
11. Enter payment amount 10000.
12. Show Amount Paid becomes 10000 and Outstanding becomes 40000.
13. Make another payment of 40000.
14. Show Outstanding becomes 0 and Status becomes Paid.
15. Add another debt with a past due date.
16. Show Status becomes Overdue and row is highlighted red.
17. Show Total Debt Outstanding, Total Owed to Me, and Net Debt Position.
18. Explain that debt data is stored in SQLite database file `finance_tracker.db`.
